# Referee report template

Fill this in as if for a journal. Be adversarial: the purpose of these rounds
is to find what a real referee would find, before a real referee does. A report
that recommends acceptance without major points has failed at its job.

## Summary (3 lines)

What the paper claims, in your own words. If you cannot state it in three
lines, that is itself a major point.

## Recommendation

[ ] Accept  [ ] Minor revision  [ ] Major revision  [ ] Reject

## Major points

Number them M1, M2, ... Each must give:
- the location (section number, equation label, figure number);
- what is wrong, unsupported, or missing;
- the specific change that would resolve it.

A major point is one that, left unaddressed, would make you recommend against
publication.

## Minor points

Number them m1, m2, ... Same format, but for issues that do not affect the
validity of the results.

## Questions the authors must answer

Points where you cannot tell from the manuscript whether the result is right.

## Referee personas

**Referee A, interacting-vacuum theorist (Bruni/Wands school).**
Focus: the covariance of the energy transfer; the choice of foliation and
whether it is a physical assumption or a convenience; the gauge used for
perturbations; whether the positivity theorem is genuinely new or a restatement
of the obvious; whether the claimed relation to Wands, De-Santiago and Wang
(arXiv:1203.6776) and to the interacting-vacuum perturbation formalism
(arXiv:2312.14123) is accurate; whether the phantom or sandwich statements
survive a change of anchoring; whether the four-form bridge is a derivation or
a decoration.

**Referee B, DESI-era data analyst.**
Focus: correctness of every likelihood; the treatment of the sound horizon and
of r_d, including whether using the pre-activation matter density is defensible
and clearly stated; the Pantheon+ covariance and the marginalization over the
absolute magnitude; cosmic-chronometer systematics; whether the compressed CMB
priors are legitimate for a non-standard late-time expansion, and whether the
calibration of the semi-analytic recipe is disclosed; MCMC convergence
diagnostics; fairness of the model comparison, including whether the prior
ranges bias the evidence; and whether the reported sign of the interaction is
honestly compared with the DESI-era literature that prefers the opposite sign.

**Referee C, mathematical relativist / mimetic-gravity specialist.**
Focus: well-posedness of the constrained-clock action and whether the known
mimetic pathologies (vanishing sound speed, caustics, gradient instabilities)
are confronted or evaded by silence; the exact statement and use of every
hypothesis in every theorem, and whether any theorem uses an assumption it does
not state; the four-form computation; the integrability condition and what it
forces; the Derrick argument and the stability status of the solved defect;
whether the Lindblad construction adds physics or only vocabulary; and whether
any result is presented at a higher level of the claims ladder than its proof
supports.
