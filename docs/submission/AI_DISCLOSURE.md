# Statement on the use of AI tools

This is the text to be placed in the manuscript immediately after the
Acknowledgments, as an unnumbered subsection titled "Use of artificial
intelligence tools". It is deliberately specific rather than minimal: under the
arXiv policy in force since May 2026, an understated disclosure combined with a
single unverifiable claim or reference is a far worse outcome than an
over-explicit one.

---

**Use of artificial intelligence tools.** The conceptual proposal, the physical
hypotheses, and the scientific decisions in this work are the author's. Large
language models (Anthropic Claude, through the Claude Code interface) were used
as writing and computational assistants: drafting and editing English prose,
proposing and checking steps in derivations, writing and refactoring the
numerical code, and producing internal critical reviews of the manuscript. All
analytical results were re-derived and checked by the author; every numerical
value quoted in the text and every figure is produced by the archived code
[DOI], from public data whose checksums are recorded in the repository; and
every bibliographic reference was resolved against the arXiv, INSPIRE or
Crossref record by an automated check whose log is archived with the code. No
AI system is an author, and the author takes full responsibility for the
content of this paper.

---

## Journal-specific notes

- **APS (PRD)**: AI-generated or AI-modified figures are not permitted. Every
  figure here is produced by `code/absorb/plotting/fig_*.py` from the archived
  results, which satisfies this. Substantive generative-AI use must be
  documented with the tool and the specific use case, as above.
- **IOP (JCAP, CQG)**: follows COPE. AI cannot be an author; transparency about
  generative-AI use in research or drafting is expected. AI use in peer review
  is not permitted.
- **Springer (EPJC)**: LLM use must be documented in the Methods or an
  equivalent section; AI-assisted copy editing alone is exempt. The use here
  goes beyond copy editing, so the statement is required.
