# Cover letter outline

Target: JCAP (primary). Backups, in order: Classical and Quantum Gravity;
European Physical Journal C; Physics of the Dark Universe.

## Why JCAP

No page limit, which suits a paper carrying both theorems and a data analysis;
it is the natural home of the interacting-vacuum and DESI-era interacting dark
energy literature that this work must be read against; and it tolerates a
theorem-and-proof presentation, which several astronomy journals do not.

## Structure

**Paragraph 1, what the paper is.** One kinetic mechanism, irreversible
absorption of a pressureless precursor into dark matter and a growing vacuum
with a rate that depends on the accumulated conversion and on a causal memory
of it, developed as an effective framework with explicitly separated levels of
claim.

**Paragraph 2, what is new.** Three items, no more:

1. The precursor-reservoir structure makes positivity automatic, which is
   exactly what fails in seven of the eight interacting dark energy models
   catalogued as pathological in the recent literature; and the model maps onto
   a specific, restricted corner of the standard parametrizations rather than
   reproducing them.
2. Memory produces hysteresis: two universes with identical densities today but
   different memory states have different futures. No coupling of the form
   Q(rho_dm, rho_de, H), including sign-switching ones, has this property, and
   it shows up in the snap parameter while cancelling out of the jerk.
3. The one-way model predicts j >= 1 + 2 Omega_r, which is the opposite sign
   from running vacuum and from the DESI-preferred CPL quadrant, so the sign of
   j - 1 is a clean discriminator. The paper confronts this with data rather
   than asserting it.

**Paragraph 3, relation to prior art.** State plainly that the constrained-clock
action is the Lim-Sawicki-Vikman construction, that interacting vacuum
cosmology is due to Wands and collaborators, and that a recent paper already
combines mimetic dynamics with an interacting vacuum and a DESI fit. What is
added is the kinetic law and the memory variable, and the paper says so.

**Paragraph 4, the honest caveat.** DESI-era fits weakly prefer the opposite
sign of the dark-sector coupling. The paper reports this, treats the one-way
model as falsifiable rather than favoured, and analyses the reverse-channel
extension alongside it.

**Paragraph 5, practicalities.** Data and code availability with the DOI; the
AI-use statement; suggested referees (to be chosen by the author); no
conflicts of interest.

## What not to do

Do not describe the paper as a unified theory or as a theory of everything in
the cover letter or the abstract. The manuscript classifies every result as an
identity, a theorem of the model, or a conjecture, and the cover letter should
reflect that discipline. An editor who reads an overclaim in the first
paragraph does not reach the second.
