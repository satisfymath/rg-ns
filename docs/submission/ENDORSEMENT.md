# arXiv endorsement plan

## Why this is needed

Since January 2026 arXiv requires new submitters to be endorsed in the target
archive; an institutional email address is no longer sufficient on its own.
The automatic path requires prior authorship on an accepted arXiv paper in the
same domain. With no prior arXiv paper, a personal endorsement from an
established author is required.

## Archive choice

**Primary: astro-ph.CO. Cross-list: gr-qc.**

The observational section anchors the paper as late-time cosmology
phenomenology, which is squarely astro-ph.CO. Submitting a manuscript with this
scope primarily to gr-qc carries a higher risk of reclassification, because a
broad framework paper without a data section reads as speculative there. One
endorsement suffices; the cross-list does not require a second.

## Who to ask

Candidates should be active authors in interacting dark energy, interacting
vacuum, or DESI-era dark-sector constraints. Draw from two pools:

1. Cosmology groups in Chile (Universidad de Chile, Pontificia Universidad
   Catolica, Universidad de Santiago, Universidad Andres Bello), which have the
   advantage of a shared timezone and language for follow-up.
2. Two international authors working directly on the interacting vacuum
   lineage or on DESI DR2 dark-sector constraints.

Identify five candidates and approach them one at a time, not in parallel.

## What to send

A short email containing, in this order:

1. One paragraph on what the paper does: a kinetic mechanism with memory for
   the dark sector, with exact theorems, a falsifiable cosmographic prediction,
   and a fit to DESI DR2, Pantheon+ and cosmic chronometers.
2. The PDF.
3. The link to the code and data repository with its DOI.
4. The AI-use statement, quoted in full, up front rather than buried. An
   endorser is putting their name behind a new author; being told plainly how
   the manuscript was produced is the courteous and the safer approach.
5. The explicit note that no reply is expected beyond yes or no, and that the
   author is not asking for a review.

## Timing

Request endorsement only after every gate in `make check` passes, the three
adversarial referee rounds are closed, and the PDF is final. An endorser who
opens a draft with broken references will decline, and there is no second
chance with the same person.
