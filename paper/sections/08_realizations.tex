\section{Covariant realizations of a silent vacuum}
\label{sec:realizations}

\Cref{asm:closure} postulates a sector with $p=-\rho$ that receives energy. This
section asks what can actually produce such a sector. Three constructions are
examined, and it is important to say at the outset that they establish
different things: a four-form gives an exact vacuum tensor with no local
propagating modes; a constrained clock gives a covariant action whose field
equations reproduce the background transfer; and neither, on its own, produces
the reinforced conversion law. The last subsection proposes the bridge between
them, at the level of a conjecture with a stated test.

\subsection{Absence of modes is not absence of a tensor}\label{sec:realizations-four-form}

\begin{proposition}[Four-form vacuum]\label{prop:four-form}
  Let $C_{\mu\nu\rho}$ be a three-form potential with
  $\fourform_{\mu\nu\rho\sigma}=4\covd_{[\mu}C_{\nu\rho\sigma]}$ and
  %
  \begin{equation}\label{eq:realizations-four-form-action}
    \Sact_4=-\frac{1}{2\cdot4!}\int \dd^4x\,\gdet\,
    \fourform_{\alpha\beta\gamma\delta}\fourform^{\alpha\beta\gamma\delta} .
  \end{equation}
  %
  In a connected source-free region the field equations imply
  %
  \begin{keyresult}{I}{eq:realizations-four-form}
    \fourform_{\mu\nu\rho\sigma}=h\,\epsilon_{\mu\nu\rho\sigma},
    \qquad \partial_\mu h=0,
    \qquad T^{(4)}_{\mu\nu}=-\frac{h^2}{2}g_{\mu\nu} .
  \end{keyresult}
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
  Varying $C$ and integrating by parts gives
  $\covd_\mu\fourform^{\mu\nu\rho\sigma}=0$. In four dimensions every
  four-form is proportional to the volume form, and
  $\covd_\mu\epsilon^{\mu\nu\rho\sigma}=0$, so $h$ is constant --- a statement
  about the non-dynamical four-form; for the version in which the flux itself
  is promoted to a degree of freedom see
  \textcite{albertini2023_230312842}. Varying the
  metric before imposing the solution gives
  $T^{(4)}_{\mu\nu}=\tfrac{1}{3!}\fourform_{\mu\alpha\beta\gamma}
  \fourform_\nu{}^{\alpha\beta\gamma}
  -\tfrac{1}{2\cdot4!}g_{\mu\nu}\fourform^2$; with signature $(-+++)$ the
  contractions are
  $\epsilon_{\mu\alpha\beta\gamma}\epsilon_\nu{}^{\alpha\beta\gamma}=-3!\,g_{\mu\nu}$
  and $\epsilon_{\alpha\beta\gamma\delta}\epsilon^{\alpha\beta\gamma\delta}=-4!$,
  giving $T_{\mu\nu}=-h^2g_{\mu\nu}+\tfrac12h^2g_{\mu\nu}$, which is
  \cref{eq:realizations-four-form} with $\rho_4=h^2/2$ and $p_4=-h^2/2$.
\end{proof}

A massless three-form gauge field has $\binom{D-2}{3}$ local polarizations,
which vanishes for $D=4$: its only amplitude is the integration constant $h$.
This settles a question that recurs in discussions of a dark sector: a
component can be electromagnetically silent, carry no local waves, and still
determine the expansion. It does not settle the magnitude of $h$, and it does
not produce conversion, because in the free theory $h$ cannot change.

\subsection{An obstruction for a canonical order parameter}
\label{sec:realizations-obstruction}

It is tempting to identify the converted fraction with a rolling scalar. That
identification is not available.

\begin{proposition}[No rolling exact vacuum]\label{prop:no-rolling}
  For a canonical real scalar with
  $\Lag=-\tfrac12(\covd\phi)^2-W(\phi)$ one has
  $\rho_\phi+p_\phi=\dot\phi^2$. Hence $w_\phi=-1$ exactly requires
  $\dot\phi=0$.
\end{proposition}

So one cannot simultaneously impose $\phi\propto \fconv$, $\dot \fconv>0$ and
$p_\phi=-\rho_\phi$ with a non-degenerate kinetic term. This is why $\fconv$ is
kept throughout as a statistical fraction and $\rhoV$ as a separate variable of
the effective description, rather than the two being identified. A double-well
potential $W(s)=W_0+\lambda_W s^2(1-s)^2-\Delta W(3s^2-2s^3)$ with
$\lambda_W>3\Delta W>0$ has minima at $s=0,1$ with $W(1)-W(0)=-\Delta W$, so
the transition releases energy; but releasing
energy is not the same as increasing a vacuum density, and a barrier picture
alone yields no rate. A semiclassical nucleation rate would require a critical
configuration, its action and a prefactor, and a rate per four-volume must be
converted to a rate per region before it can be compared with $\Gam$, which has
dimensions of inverse time.

\subsection{A constrained clock}\label{sec:realizations-clock}

Introduce a scalar $\clock$ of dimension $-1$ and a multiplier $\lagm$:
%
\begin{keyresult}{A}{eq:realizations-clock-action}
  \Sact_{\rm clock}=\int \dd^4x\,\gdet\left[\frac{\Mpl^2}{2}R
  -\frac{\lagm}{2}\left(g^{\mu\nu}\partial_\mu \clock\partial_\nu \clock+1\right)
  -V(\clock)\right] .
\end{keyresult}

\begin{proposition}[Clock field equations]\label{prop:clock}
  On the branch with future-directed timelike gradient and $\lagm\ge0$,
  \cref{eq:realizations-clock-action} gives
  %
  \begin{keyresult}{I}{eq:realizations-clock}
    \ulow=-\partial_\mu \clock,\quad u^\mu u_\mu=-1,\quad
    \Tmn=\lagm\, u_\mu u_\nu-V g_{\mu\nu},
  \end{keyresult}
  %
  together with $u^\nu\covd_\nu \umu=0$,
  $\covd_\mu(\lagm \umu)=-V_{,\clock}$ and
  $\covd_\mu T_V^{\mu\nu}=V_{,\clock}\,u^\nu$.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
  Varying $\lagm$ imposes $(\partial \clock)^2=-1$. The metric variation
  produces $\lagm\partial_\mu \clock\partial_\nu \clock$, and on the constraint
  the matter Lagrangian equals $-V$. Varying $\clock$ gives
  $\covd_\mu(\lagm\covd^\mu \clock)=V_{,\clock}$, and since
  $\covd^\mu \clock=-\umu$ this is the stated continuity equation. The symmetry
  of $\covd_\mu\covd_\nu \clock$ gives
  $u^\nu\covd_\nu u_\mu=\tfrac12\covd_\mu(u^\nu u_\nu)=0$, so the flow is
  geodesic.
\end{proof}

In FLRW one may take $\clock=t$, and then $\dot\lagm+3\Hub\lagm=-\dot V$ with
$3\Mpl^2\Hub^2=\lagm+V$: an exact realization of dust transferring energy to
the vacuum whenever $V_{,\clock}\ge0$. This construction is not new. It is the
"dust of dark energy" of \textcite{lim2010_10035751}, and with a potential it
is mimetic matter in the sense of
\textcite{chamseddine2014_14033961}; reviews are given by
\textcite{sebastiani2016_161208661} and \textcite{malaeb2026_260214082}. A
recent construction combines a mimetic-like silent sector with an interacting
vacuum and fits it to DESI data \parencite{prez2026_260719494}. We use
it here as an existence proof for the background of
\cref{sec:homogeneous-cosmology}, not as a claim of novelty.

\subsection{The potential is determined, not chosen}\label{sec:realizations-determined}

The standard objection to \cref{eq:realizations-clock-action} is that
$V(\clock)$ can be reverse-engineered to reproduce almost any expansion
history, so the construction explains nothing
\parencite{chamseddine2014_14033961}. We do not claim to answer it. What can be
said is weaker and worth stating precisely.

\begin{proposition}[Kinetically determined potential]\label{prop:determined-V}
  Fix $(\Gamz,\alpha,\beta,\tauM,\bfrac)$, the activation law, and initial
  data. By \cref{thm:regular-future} the background is unique, so with
  $\clock=t$,
  %
  \begin{keyresult}{I}{eq:realizations-determined-V}
    V(\clock)=V_0+(1-\bfrac)\int_{t_0}^{\clock}\Gam\rhoI\,\dd t ,
    \qquad
    \lagm=\rhoI+\rhoD ,
  \end{keyresult}
  %
  is a five-parameter family, monotone non-decreasing and bounded above by
  \cref{eq:cosmology-bounds}.
\end{proposition}

\Cref{eq:realizations-determined-V} is simply the integral of the third line of
\cref{eq:cosmology-system}; the content is in what it is being integrated
against. The kinetic law is an additional postulate imposed from outside the
action, so the action still does not constrain $V$, and the mimetic objection
stands. What has been gained is that the ansatz for $V$ has five parameters
rather than the freedom of a function space, is monotone non-decreasing and
bounded above by \cref{eq:cosmology-bounds}, carries a specified early-time
cutoff, and was not chosen by looking at any dataset. That is a statement about
naturalness and about the number of free functions, not a derivation, and it is
what makes the fits of \cref{sec:observations} interpretable and the prediction
of \cref{sec:cosmography} falsifiable.

One further consequence of the same limitation should be recorded here rather
than left for the reader to notice. The matter tensor of
\cref{eq:realizations-clock} is $\lagm u_\mu u_\nu-\rhoV g_{\mu\nu}$ with
$\lagm=\rhoI+\rhoD$: the action distinguishes the dust sector from the vacuum,
but nothing in it distinguishes the precursor from the defects. Since $\bfrac$
is also degenerate with $\epsilon$ at background level (\cref{prop:ide-map})
and cancels from linear growth (\cref{thm:b-independence}), the split of the
dark sector into $\rhoI$ and $\rhoD$ is bookkeeping at every level this paper
computes. It acquires content only through the defect sector of
\cref{sec:defects}, and only if those objects are real.

Two known problems of this class of theories remain, and both must be stated
without softening.

The scalar sound speed vanishes identically. This single fact has two faces
that appear in different places in this paper and should not be counted twice:
it means there is no scalar sound horizon, and it is also the reason the linear
system of \cref{sec:perturbations} has no $k$-dependent coefficient. The latter
is what makes \cref{prop:no-instability} available, but it is a degeneracy of
the scalar sector rather than a restoring force, so it establishes the absence
of one specific instability and nothing more.

Pressureless geodesic flow develops caustics, which no linear analysis can
exclude \parencite{babichev2017_170403367}. \Cref{eq:realizations-clock-action}
contains no higher-derivative term of the form $(\Box \clock)^2$, so the
gradient instabilities identified by \textcite{ijjas2016_160408586} in extended
mimetic models are absent \emph{from this action}. That scoping matters: the
action alone leaves $V(\clock)$ arbitrary and contains no rate law, so it is
not by itself the theory whose perturbations are computed in
\cref{sec:perturbations} and fitted in \cref{sec:observations}. The additional
prescription those sections use, \cref{asm:geodesic}, is not a consequence of
\cref{eq:realizations-clock-action}, and its scalar sector has not been shown
free of gradient instabilities by the argument just given. Caustics are taken up
where they actually bite, in \cref{sec:perturbations-realization}.

\subsection{Nucleation events as absorption events}\label{sec:realizations-bridge}

The three constructions above are related by more than analogy, and this
subsection states the connection precisely enough to be attacked.

In a four-form sector coupled to membranes, nucleation of a membrane of charge
$\mathsf{q}$ shifts the flux across its worldvolume, $h\to h\pm \mathsf{q}$, so
the vacuum density jumps by $\Delta V=\pm h\mathsf{q}+\mathsf{q}^2/2$
\parencite{brown1987_dynamical,brown1988_neutralization,bousso2000_hep-th0004134,kaloper2023_230502349}.
Identify the
upward event with one absorption event: a precursor unit of energy $E_p$ per
patch volume is consumed, with the accounting
$E_p=\Delta V\,\mathcal V_p+E_{\rm wall}$, where $E_{\rm wall}$ is the energy
that does not end as vacuum. The symbol is $E_p$ and not $\epsilon$ because
$\epsilon$ is the convertible fraction everywhere else in this paper and the
two are unrelated. The branching ratio is then not a free parameter but a ratio
of energies, $\bfrac=E_{\rm wall}/E_p$, which is what the cosmological sections
use.

\begin{remark}[The remnant is not the defect of \cref{sec:defects}, and is not
  stable]\label{rem:bridge-limits}
  It is tempting to read $E_{\rm wall}$ as the rest energy of a localized
  object and to identify that object with the texture of \cref{sec:defects}.
  Neither step is available here, and saying so costs the bridge half its
  reach.

  First, the thin-wall membrane leaves no object. For a closed wall of radius
  $R$ enclosing a region lower in vacuum energy by $\Delta V$, the energy
  $E=4\pi R^2\varsigma-\tfrac43\pi R^3\Delta V$ has its only non-zero
  stationary point at $R_c=2\varsigma/\Delta V$, where
  $E''=-8\pi\varsigma<0$. That is the critical bubble, a maximum: the wall
  either collapses or runs away, and $E(R_c)=16\pi\varsigma^3/3(\Delta V)^2$ is
  a barrier, not a rest mass. A stable remnant needs an ingredient this action
  does not contain --- angular momentum, a conserved charge on the wall, a
  repulsive core --- and adding one changes the theory rather than interpreting
  it. Particle-like remnants have been constructed in three-form sectors
  \parencite{khan2026_260403363}, but with additional structure, not from the
  action written here.

  Second, even granting a remnant, it would not be the object of
  \cref{sec:defects}. That object is a map $S^3\to S^3$ built from an
  $\mathrm{SU}(2)$-valued scalar, carrying a degree in $\pi_3(S^3)$ and given
  its size by the quartic term of \cref{eq:defects-energy}. The membrane is a
  domain wall carrying a gauge charge under a three-form potential, which has
  no propagating modes in four dimensions. The fields differ, the charge groups
  differ, and nothing sets the size of the second. They are independent
  constructions, and their coexistence in this paper is not a mechanism.

  What the bridge does connect is the \emph{kinetics}: the event sequence, the
  reinforcement coefficient of \cref{eq:realizations-alpha}, and the
  interpretation of $\bfrac$ as the fraction of transferred energy that does
  not reach the vacuum. Where that fraction goes --- radiation, gravitational
  radiation, or collapse --- this paper does not determine.
\end{remark}

\begin{remark}[The event sequence is a reinforced birth process]
  \label{prop:bridge}
  If each of $N$ precursor units per patch can nucleate once, and the
  $(k+1)$-th event has rate $\Gam_k=\Gam(h_0+k\mathsf{q})$, then $K_t$ is
  exactly the chain of \cref{eq:process-intensity} with
  $\Gam(\fconv)=\Gam(h_0+\fconv N\mathsf{q})$. The exponential law
  \cref{eq:process-rate} corresponds to $\log\Gam$ linear in $h$ over the
  relevant range, with
  $\alpha=N\mathsf{q}\,\dd\log\Gam/\dd h$. Reinforcement, $\alpha>0$, holds if
  and only if the upward tunnelling exponent decreases with accumulated flux.

  The identification is immediate once each unit may nucleate at most once: the
  number of units still able to nucleate after $k$ events is $N-k$, and each
  carries the common rate $\Gam(h_0+k\mathsf q)$, so the total intensity is
  $\lambda_k=(N-k)\Gam(h_0+k\mathsf q)$, which is
  \cref{eq:process-intensity} with $\fconv=k/N$. The exponential form
  \cref{eq:process-rate} is then the statement that $\log\Gam$ is linear in $h$
  over the range swept, with the stated identification of $\alpha$. We label
  this a remark rather than a proposition because the premises -- one
  nucleation per unit, a common rate, a flux that shifts by a fixed quantum --
  are modelling choices, not consequences of the four-form dynamics.
\end{remark}

Whether this is a constraint or a picture turns on the sign, and the sign needs
care. The \dS entropy is $S=8\pi^2\Mpl^2/\Hub^2=24\pi^2\Mpl^4/V$, which
\emph{decreases} with $V$. An upward nucleation therefore lowers the horizon
entropy and is exponentially suppressed relative to the downward one,
$\Gam_\uparrow/\Gam_\downarrow=e^{-\Delta S}$ with $\Delta S>0$: the naive
reading, that accumulating flux is entropically favoured, is backwards. What
survives is one order more delicate. The suppression of a single upward step,
%
\begin{keyresult}{X}{eq:realizations-suppression}
  \Delta S=S(V)-S(V+\mathsf q h)
  \simeq 24\pi^2\Mpl^4\,\frac{\mathsf q h}{V^2} ,
\end{keyresult}
%
itself decreases as $V$ grows. That is not yet the statement wanted, because
$\Delta S$ controls the \emph{ratio}: writing
$\Gam_\uparrow=\Gam_\downarrow e^{-\Delta S}$,
%
\begin{equation}\label{eq:realizations-rate-split}
  \odv{\log\Gam_\uparrow}{h}
  = \odv{\log\Gam_\downarrow}{h} - \odv{\Delta S}{h} ,
\end{equation}
%
and a downward rate falling faster than the suppression relaxes would carry the
upward rate down with it. The second term is the one \cref{eq:realizations-suppression}
supplies; the first has to be computed.

Take the downward nucleation in the thin-wall limit,
$B_\downarrow=27\pi^2\varsigma^4/2(\mathsf qh)^3$ with $\varsigma$ the membrane
tension, valid while the critical bubble is small against the horizon, which
requires $\varsigma\lesssim(\sqrt6/3)\Mpl\mathsf q$. Both terms then scale the
same way in the flux,
%
\begin{keyresult}{X}{eq:realizations-lnrate}
  \log\Gam_\uparrow=-\frac{\mathcal K}{h^{3}},
  \qquad
  \mathcal K=\frac{\pi^2}{\mathsf q^{3}}
    \left(96\Mpl^4\mathsf q^4+\tfrac{27}{2}\varsigma^4\right)>0 ,
\end{keyresult}
%
so $\dd\log\Gam_\uparrow/\dd h=3\mathcal K/h^4>0$ and the upward rate does rise
with accumulated flux --- now for a reason, and a stronger one than the
original argument gave, since the two contributions push the same way rather
than competing. In the regime where the thin-wall form is valid the entropy
term dominates, $\mathcal K_{\rm bounce}/\mathcal K_{\rm entropy}
=9\varsigma^4/64\Mpl^4\mathsf q^4<1/16$: what makes each successive nucleation
easier is that the horizon entropy cost of a step falls as the vacuum grows.

\Cref{eq:realizations-lnrate} also says what the exponential law of
\cref{eq:process-rate} costs. Linearity of $\log\Gam$ in $h$ across a swept
range $\Delta h=N\mathsf q$ holds with relative curvature $4N\mathsf q/h$, and
truncating at the linear term leaves a relative error $2N\mathsf q/h$ in the
exponent at the end of conversion: the form is an approximation valid when the
flux converted is small against the flux already there. The coefficient is then
%
\begin{keyresult}{X}{eq:realizations-alpha}
  \alpha=3\,\frac{N\mathsf q}{h}\,\Delta S
  \left[1+\order{(\varsigma/\Mpl\mathsf q)^4}\right],
  \qquad\text{so}\qquad
  \alpha<\tfrac34\Delta S ,
\end{keyresult}
%
the bound following from the linearity condition. That is a relation rather
than a fit: reinforcement of order unity requires an entropy change per step of
at least order unity, and the two cannot be chosen independently.

These two results carry level X, and the reason is worth stating rather than
leaving to the table. They are proved, and the algebra is checked
symbolically, but they are proved \emph{about a different system}: a four-form
with charged membranes, in the thin-wall limit, with detailed balance between
the two nucleation directions, one nucleation per unit, a common rate, and a
flux shifting by a fixed quantum. Several of those are modelling choices
rather than consequences of the four-form dynamics, as
\cref{rem:bridge-limits} says, and detailed balance is an equilibrium relation
that the driven nucleation assumed here does not satisfy. A result of that
kind is not a theorem of the model, and neither is it a conjecture: the
calculation is done, and it is the identification that is open. Level C would
claim the first, level S would deny the second.

Two honest caveats accompany it. At vacuum densities of order the observed one
these rates are astronomically small, so the mechanism can only be relevant if
the nucleation is not thermal but driven by the precursor, which is precisely
what \cref{asm:closure} assumes and does not derive. And the bridge covers
$\alpha$ and $\bfrac$ but not the memory sector: nothing in this subsection
produces $\beta$ or $\tauM$. We therefore record \cref{prop:bridge} as a
conditional statement about the structure of the process, and the claim that
this mechanism operates in nature as a conjecture, listed as such in
\cref{sec:outlook}.

The same bridge identifies what the reverse channel of
\cref{sec:positivity-ide} would be: an ordinary downward nucleation, returning
vacuum energy to the localized sector.

\paragraph{What this section establishes.}
That a silent vacuum tensor exists (\cref{prop:four-form}), that a covariant
action reproduces the background transfer (\cref{prop:clock}), and that its
potential is fixed by the kinetics rather than chosen
(\cref{prop:determined-V}). What it does not establish is a microscopic
derivation of the rate law, the value of $\alpha$, or that caustics of the
clock congruence are benign.
