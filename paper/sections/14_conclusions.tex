\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conclusions}

A single kinetic idea has been followed through to its consequences. Regions of
a substrate convert irreversibly; the conversion reinforces itself; the
reinforcement responds to a lagged memory of what has already converted; and
the products are a pressureless component and a growing vacuum. The question
was what that implies once written as equations.

At the level of the kinetics, the population-level memory law is exactly the average of a
per-unit relaxation law, so it is equivalent to a local assumption rather
than an extra non-local one, and the pair
$(\fconv,\Mmem)$ is Markov even though $\fconv$ alone is not
(\cref{prop:memory-origin}). The finite-population process has a controlled
deterministic limit (\cref{thm:deterministic-limit}); the $N^{-1/2}$ law for
its fluctuations is exact where the rate does not depend on the state, and is
supported by direct simulation where it does. Its distinguishing property is
path dependence: two histories reaching the same converted fraction with
different memory states convert at different epochs, and therefore accumulate
different vacuum energies (\cref{thm:hysteresis}). An
interaction written as a function of the densities and the expansion rate,
including one whose sign switches with redshift, closes on those densities, so
they fix its entire future and it has no such freedom. The difference is the
dimension of the state space, not the shape of the coupling.

Coupled to general relativity under an explicit closure, the model has exact
identities relating the converted fraction to the surviving densities, global
positivity and bounded expansion, and a \dS attractor reached without the
Hubble rate ever increasing (\cref{thm:regular-future,thm:de-sitter}).
Positivity holds structurally, because every outflow is proportional to the
density of the sector it leaves, which is the property that the
recipient-proportional couplings among those catalogued as pathological lack
(\cref{thm:quasi-positivity,prop:recipient-proportional}). The model maps onto a restricted corner of the
standard parametrizations and never onto the other family
(\cref{prop:ide-map}).

Three consequences of the one-way model are falsifiable and cannot be tuned
away within it. The effective dark matter can only lose energy, never gain it
(\cref{thm:no-sign-flip}); adding the reverse channel of
\cref{sec:positivity-track-b} removes that prediction, and is a different
model, which is why it is carried through the comparison rather than mentioned
and dropped. The jerk
parameter satisfies $\jerk\ge1+2\Omr$, which has the opposite sign to both
running vacuum and the currently favoured evolving dark-energy quadrant
(\cref{thm:jerk,thm:sign-table}). And linear growth is enhanced while the
velocity field that redshift-space distortions measure is suppressed
(\cref{eq:perturbations-growth-signature}). The memory parameters, invisible in
the jerk, reappear in the snap, although mixed there with the activation
profile (\cref{thm:memory-fingerprint}).

Confronted with Pantheon+, DESI DR2, cosmic chronometers and the Planck
distance priors, the model is not preferred, and we report that as the result
it is. $\LCDM$ wins on the Bayesian evidence against every extension fitted
here (\cref{tab:comparison}). The convertible fraction $\epsilon$ cannot be
measured at all --- the rate may always vanish, so no value of $\epsilon$ is
excluded --- and the quantity that does carry information, the matter deficit,
is bounded by \cref{eq:observations-deficit-limit}. The jerk excess is bounded
separately, by profiling it directly: the deficit does not constrain it, the
relation between the two being a floor whose hypothesis this model's activation
law fails to satisfy (\cref{sec:cosmography-jerk}). It too lies far below what
late-time cosmography can resolve (\cref{eq:observations-jerk-limit}). That
speaks to the jerk alone. The no-gain result is a statement about the sign of a
quantity no present measurement isolates, and the growth signature is not
confronted with redshift-space data here at all, so of the three sign
predictions above, one is measured and out of reach and the other two are
untested. They remain refutable in principle, which is the most that can honestly
be claimed for them today.

Along the way two defects of the earlier version of this work were found and
repaired rather than quietly dropped. The rate law with a positive floor has no
viable early universe (\cref{thm:no-go}), and the minimal repair is an
activation switch whose exponent must exceed the donor's dilution rate: $p>1$
for the constant-rate law fitted here, $p>3$ for the expansion-tracking
benchmark. And the growth equation previously
written for the precursor contrast is wrong when both products are present; the
correct variable is the total dust contrast, in which the branching ratio
cancels identically (\cref{thm:b-independence}).

What has \emph{not} been shown should be stated with equal clarity. There is no
microscopic derivation of the rate law; \cref{sec:realizations-bridge} proposes
a mechanism and \cref{sec:outlook} lists the calculation that would settle it.
The clock congruence forms caustics, and the earlier conjecture that defect
formation regulates them is now settled in the negative for the model as
published: a rate bounded on the approach cannot change the exponent of the
divergence, only its amplitude, and this model's rate is bounded
(\cref{thm:bounded-rate-obstruction}). A compression-driven rate can change the
exponent, but only above a threshold and only with no branching, which
contradicts what a relic abundance requires; and every covariant realization we
could construct runs into the dichotomy set out in
\cref{sec:defects-caustics}, which we leave unresolved. What replaces the conjecture is therefore an
obstruction, which is a weaker claim about the model and a stronger statement
about what would have to be true for it to work. The localized configuration
of \cref{sec:defects} is solved but not quantised, and its identification with
dark matter is a conjecture, not a result. Quantum mechanics is used, not
derived. Electromagnetism and the Standard Model are untouched, and the
degree-of-freedom counting in \cref{sec:outlook-em} shows they will not emerge
from a change of variables.

The claim this paper is prepared to defend is therefore narrow and, we think,
worth making: \emph{there exists an effective model of self-reinforced
absorption with memory whose cosmological consequences are exact under stated
hypotheses, whose positivity is structural rather than tuned, and which makes
three sign predictions that current and near-future data can refute.} The
identification of its ingredients with the physical universe, and any
unification of gravity with quantum theory, remain what they were before this
paper: open.
