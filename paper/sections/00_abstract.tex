\begin{abstract}
\noindent
We develop a single kinetic law for the dark sector and follow it to its
consequences. Regions of an effective substrate convert irreversibly into a
pressureless localized product and a growing vacuum energy, at a rate that
increases with the fraction already converted and with a causal memory of that
history. We show that the population-level memory law is exactly the
average of a per-unit relaxation law with a common relaxation time, so it is
equivalent to a local assumption --- one of equal or greater strength, not a
weaker one --- rather than to an extra non-local one, that the
finite-population process has a controlled deterministic limit, with an exactly
binomial $N^{-1/2}$ law where the rate does not depend on the state and
numerical evidence for the same scaling where it does, and that memory makes the kinetics path dependent: two
histories that reach the same converted fraction with different memory states
convert at different epochs and, integrated along a prescribed expansion
history, accumulate different vacuum energies.
An interaction written as a function of the densities and the expansion rate
closes on those densities, so they fix its entire future whatever its
functional form and however its sign behaves with redshift; here they do not,
because the memory is an independent state variable.

Coupled to general relativity, the model has exact identities relating the
converted fraction to the surviving densities, global positivity with bounded
expansion, and a de Sitter attractor reached without the Hubble rate ever
increasing. Positivity is structural rather than tuned, because every outflow
is proportional to the density of the sector it leaves; this is the property
lacking in the recipient-proportional couplings among those recently catalogued
as admitting negative densities. We give the exact map onto the standard
parametrizations, prove that in the one-way model the effective dark matter can only lose energy,
and derive a parameter-free cosmographic inequality, $j \ge 1 + 2\Omega_r$,
whose sign is opposite to that of running vacuum and of the currently favoured
evolving dark-energy quadrant. The memory parameters cancel from the jerk and
reappear in the snap, though not in isolation: the snap constrains their lag
together with the activation profile. In linear theory the branching ratio between the two products
cancels identically, and under the geodesic realization of the transfer ---
which we show is forced rather than chosen, since a pressureless donor whose
only source points along its own flow cannot be accelerated --- the model
predicts enhanced density growth together with suppressed velocity growth, a
signature of opposite signs that a coupling proportional to the local density
cannot produce.

Two defects of an earlier version of this work are corrected rather than
dropped: a rate with a positive floor admits no viable early universe, which we
prove and repair with an activation law; and the growth equation previously
written for the precursor contrast is wrong whenever both products are present.
Three obstructions are recorded rather than passed over. The conjecture that
conversion regularizes the caustics of the clock congruence is false for the
model as published, because a rate bounded on the approach cannot change the
exponent of the divergence. The branching ratio required for regularity there
vanishes, while a relic abundance requires it not to. And the
compression-driven rate that would repair the first runs into a covariant
obstruction we leave unresolved. These are not three independent failures: all
three, and the forced geodesic realization with them, follow from the one
hypothesis that also supplies the model's only source of acceleration, namely
that the recipient is an exact vacuum receiving energy with no momentum
transfer, so that what a successor theory would have to change is identified
even though it is not built here. A cosmographic bound relating the matter
deficit to the jerk is also shown not to apply to this model, its hypothesis on
the transfer profile failing for every activation redshift.
Covariant realizations are exhibited for the vacuum sector, including a
four-form whose membrane nucleation events map onto absorption events, and the
constrained-clock potential is fixed by the kinetics to a five-parameter,
monotone, bounded family rather than a free function, which narrows but does
not remove the standard objection to mimetic constructions. A
finite-energy unit-charge defect is solved numerically, and an open quantum
realization reproduces the classical chain with a mean-field closure error
measured to scale as $N^{-1}$, subleading to the intrinsic $N^{-1/2}$
fluctuations. The model is confronted with Pantheon+, DESI DR2 baryon acoustic
oscillations, cosmic chronometers and the Planck distance priors, and the
outcome is a null result reported as one: $\LCDM$ is preferred, the convertible
fraction is not measurable because the rate can always vanish, and what the
data do bound is the matter deficit, to below
$\numProfileXiHBaselineMatterDeficitLimitNV$ of the critical density.
Profiling the predicted jerk excess directly bounds it more than an order of
magnitude below the resolution of present late-time data, so the model's sign
predictions are refutable in principle and not yet in practice.

Every displayed key result carries an evidential level, from postulate through
identity and theorem to conjecture, printed beside it and checked for presence
and placement as part of the build. We do not
derive quantum mechanics, electromagnetism, or any Standard-Model content, and
we do not claim that the defects are the dark matter. What we claim is narrower
and testable: an effective model of self-reinforced absorption with memory,
with exact cosmological consequences under stated hypotheses and three sign
predictions that data can refute.
\end{abstract}

\medskip
\noindent\textbf{Keywords:} interacting vacuum; dark sector; absorbing-state
kinetics; memory and hysteresis; cosmography; mimetic gravity; topological
defects.
